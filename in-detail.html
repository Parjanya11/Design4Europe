Design4Europe

This campaign is coordinated by the Faculty of Industrial Design Engineering at Delft University of Technology, in partnership with BEDA, CreativeFED, ekip, CLICKNL, the Dutch Design Foundation, and the Danish Design Centre. Together, these organisations have initiated a European coalition to strengthen the role of design in FP10 and future European policies. We invite organisations from all European regions to join this growing network.
