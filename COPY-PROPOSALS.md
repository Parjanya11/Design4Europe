# Copy proposals — for Hilbrand and Jeroen to decide

**Status: proposal only. Nothing here is live on the site.**

The live copy still makes the original ask. This document sets out an
alternative framing for the hero and The ask, for you to accept, amend or
reject. No change will be made without your say-so.

---

## Why propose a change at all

The site currently argues that Design *should* be given a role in FP10.

Since it was written, Parliament's compromise amendment has appeared, and it
already names design in the draft text — creative and design methods, design
in the innovation journey, artistic research methodologies, the NEB Facility,
the SSH-A pilot.

That changes what is being asked for. The campaign is no longer asking for
something new to be added. It is asking that something already written is not
removed during trilogue negotiations in December and January.

That is a stronger position, for two reasons:

1. **It is easier to join.** People mobilise more readily to defend something
   concrete than to request something hypothetical.
2. **It is verifiable.** The draft text can be quoted, and now is, in The case.

The risk of *not* changing it: a reader who knows the amendment may conclude
the site is out of date, which undercuts everything else on the page.

## Proposal A — Hero

**Current**

> Europe cannot function without Design.
>
> Give Design a structural role in FP10, the Union's next research and
> innovation programme. The architecture is being decided now, and Design must
> be built into it.

**Proposed**

> Europe cannot function without Design.
>
> Parliament's draft text for FP10 already names Design. Trilogue negotiations
> begin in December, and the text can still change. Add your name before it is
> locked.

*Keeps the headline untouched. Replaces the subtitle with the sharper,
time-bound ask.*

## Open questions

- Is "trilogue" the right word for a public audience, or should it read
  "the final negotiations between Parliament, Council and Commission"?
- Should the hero carry a deadline date, given trilogue has no fixed end?
  A date that passes without resolution makes the site look stale.
- After trilogue concludes, this framing expires. Who updates it then?
