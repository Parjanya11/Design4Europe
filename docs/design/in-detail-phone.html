DEvidence

The text behind our ask

“…include design from the early stages of the innovation journey, while promoting cultural diversity and socio-cultural value.”

Specific Programme, Art. 11b, p. 18

“…provide for the integration of social sciences, humanities and the arts (SSHA) across all components under the Programme”

Regulation, Art. 5(a), p. 27

“…promote co-creation and co-design through the engagement of citizens and civil society”

Regulation, Art. 5(cb), p. 28

What the text does not yet say: neither document names Design as a discipline of its own. That is what we ask Parliament, Council and Commission to add.
