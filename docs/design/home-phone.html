What is FP10?

The EU’s research and innovation programme for 2028 to 2034. Four pillars decide what Europe researches and funds.

Red marks where Parliament’s text names design as a sector. Most of Pillar II does not.

See where design fits C

Technology alone cannot secure the Union’s autonomy. Innovations deliver impact only when people adopt them.

That is the work of Design.

The ask

Give Design a structural position in FP10.

1

A named place in FP10’s structure, in Pillar II.

2

A supported role across its Competitiveness and Society windows.

3

Support for Design research and practice as a discipline.
