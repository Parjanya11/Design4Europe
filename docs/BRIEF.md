# design4europe.eu: brief for the two-page round (Nov 2026)

The single source of context for this round. Every Claude session (VS Code, claude.ai, Claude Design) starts by reading this file. Update it when a decision is made; don't re-explain in chat.

Deadline: live on production by **Tuesday 24 Nov, evening**. Preview for Hilbrand: **Monday 23 Nov, morning**.

## 1. Why this round

At the last stakeholder meeting (Hilbrand, Jeroen), the ask was to "take them by the hand": the site must also serve people from the design community who don't yet know what design research is, what FP10 is, or what this campaign asks for. FP10 is complex, so the journey has to be simpler and clearer.

## 2. What changes

**Two pages instead of one scroll.**

**Page 1 (`/`)**, simple, for a first-time visitor:
- Hero (unchanged in substance).
- **New: "What is FP10?"** A short intro text plus a simple visual that sets context in under a minute (what FP10 is, when it runs, why it matters now, what we ask).
- Existing sections cut down to teasers, each with a "Read more" link to its section on page 2.
- The sign-on form stays the core of page 1 and is unchanged.
- Signatories, as now.
- **New: a short FAQ at the end of page 1.**

**Page 2 (`/in-detail`)**, the nitty-gritty of FP10 and the campaign's proposition:
- **A. Design across FP10:** the four-pillar structure map. Largely OK as is (parked version from 10 Nov), checked against the texts below.
- **B. Where FP10 stands:** the timeline. Keep as is.
- **C. Where design research fits:** the biggest design task. It must visually connect back to Pillar II in A (zoom from the structure into Pillar II), then show Jeroen's reading of Pillar II (see 3).

- Ends with a band that sends readers back to the form (`/#sign`). "Add your signature" stays in the header on both pages.

## 5. Verified facts and cautions (read before writing any copy)

- **Budget: confirm with Jeroen before anything is published.**
  - The Regulation (Art. 6(1)) says **EUR 200 003 562 000 in current prices (EUR 177 010 000 000 in 2025 prices)**.
  - Pillars: I 55.48 bn · II 88.766 bn · III 36.354 bn · IV 19.404 bn. NEB Facility 500 m. SSHA pilot 10 m.
  - Jeroen's deck says €154B with pillars 44.1 / 75.8 / 38.8 / 16.3, which add up to 175. **Neither figure appears in the texts.**
  - Rule stands: no euro figures on the site (`FP10.budget` stays `null`).
- **Window names, exactly as in the SP:**
  - Competitiveness I ‘Clean Energy and Industrial Decarbonisation’
  - Competitiveness II ‘Biotechnology, **Agriculture**, Health, and Sustainable Transition’ (not "Agro")
  - Competitiveness III ‘Digital Leadership’
  - Competitiveness IV ‘Critical Raw Materials, Security and Space’
  - Society I ‘Just Green Transition’
  - Society II ‘Sustainable, Inclusive and Healthy Societies’
  - Society III ‘Inclusive Digital Societies’
  - Society IV ‘Resilient Societies’
  - Horizontal Window: Missions (5), New European Bauhaus (NEB) Facility, Metrology Partnership, pilot for an SSHA Sectoral Plan
  - Fast Track to Innovation
- **The texts write "SSHA"** (Social Sciences, Humanities and the Arts), not "SSH-A".
- **Legal status:**
  - The SP is a consultation procedure: Parliament gives an opinion and the Council adopts.
  - Write "Parliament proposes" or "Parliament's text", never "FP10 includes".
- **Neither text names Design as a discipline, sector or field of education.** The words "designer", "design research", "design education" and "transdisciplinary" do not appear. That gap is the campaign's argument. Never present a quote as "the text names Design".

## 6. Quotes, exact, with references

**Vertical (red): design as a sector**
- SP Art. 11b, Competitiveness II, topic "Cultural and Creative Industries", p. 18:
  - "Creative and design methods may also be developed and tested as inputs to industrial innovation, where they contribute to product differentiation."
  - "…include design from the early stages of the innovation journey, while promoting cultural diversity and socio-cultural value." (The sentence opens "Furthermore, the activities under this broadline may promote the collaboration between the cultural and creative industries with other industrial sectors to foster new types of innovation and…")
  - "[T]he tools, technologies and methods on which European cultural and creative sectors, organisations, creators and businesses can build. This includes artistic research methodologies and non-commercial forms of cultural and artistic creation."
    - **The live site's version of this quote is not verbatim. Fix it.**
  - "Activities may address production, capture, rendering, translation and distribution technologies across audiovisual and games production, publishing, music, design, architecture, fashion and craft…"
- SP Art. 11f, Society II, topic "Culture, Cultural Heritage, including the Cultural Heritage Cloud and Creative Sectors", p. 26: "support the vitality of its cultural and creative sectors"; "the economic and social contribution of the cultural and creative sectors in Europe".
- SP Art. 11i(b), NEB Facility, p. 29: "the transformation of neighbourhoods into sustainable, inclusive and beautiful places".
  - Regulation Recital 12, p. 8: "through sustainable, innovative, creative and inclusive design".
- SP Art. 11k, pilot SSHA Sectoral Plan, p. 30–31: a Strategic Innovation Agenda and roadmap "in form of a SSHA Sectoral Plan". Regulation Art. 15d.

**Horizontal (blue): design applied to other sectors**
- SP Art. 11e, Society I, p. 23: "circular product design"
- SP Art. 11f, Society II (Health Care Systems), p. 24: "New models for public health care should prioritise human-centred design"
- SP Art. 11f, Society II (Communities and Cities), p. 25–26: "urban design"; "the planning and design of urban spaces"
- SP Art. 11g, Society III, p. 27: "safer, more transparent and human-centred digital services"
- SP Art. 11d, Competitiveness IV, p. 22: "Safe- and sustainable-by-design materials"
- The deck's example themes (Circular design, Transition design, Smart and inclusive mobility, Healthy lifestyles, eHealth, Inclusive responsible AI, Smart materials, Systemic design, Living labs) are **our examples, not text**. Label them so.

**Programme-wide (Regulation)**
- Art. 5(a), p. 27: "provide for the integration of social sciences, humanities and the arts (SSHA) across all components under the Programme" ("and the arts" is Parliament's addition)
- Art. 5(a), p. 27: "…topics dedicated to the cultural and creative industries."
- Art. 5(cb), p. 28: "promote co-creation and co-design through the engagement of citizens and civil society"

## 8. Design decisions (23 Nov, phone first)

Design references: `docs/design/home-phone.html` and `docs/design/in-detail-phone.html`. They are static mockups at 390px wide and the source of truth for content, order and look on a phone. They are not code to copy.

**One recurring picture**
- Page 1 draws FP10 as a building: a roof reading "FP10 · 2028–2034", four pillars with Pillar II widest, and a base reading "Being negotiated now".
- Inside Pillar II sit its 11 cells, coloured by state.
- Section C on page 2 zooms into the same 11 cells as a map, so both pictures must come from the same data.
